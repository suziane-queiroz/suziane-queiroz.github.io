Projects

Bachelor's Thesis · 2026

From On-Premises Databases to the Cloud: A Comparative Analysis with AWS Services

Bachelor's thesis comparing traditional on-premises database management with cloud-based solutions on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

The work compares the two models in terms of control, costs (CapEx vs OpEx), scalability, security and operational management, and examines hybrid architectures as a balance between control and flexibility. It also covers AWS global infrastructure (Regions, Availability Zones, Edge Locations) and the main managed database services: Amazon RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB and Redshift.

The analysis is complemented by hands-on experience from guided AWS labs carried out during my Cloud Networking course, including the setup of a cloud database on Amazon RDS.
